About

Who I am.

I find the problem before I try to solve it.

Outside the work

Built through sport.

I played soccer from age six through high school and ran cross country during the same years. Cross country taught me more about persistence than most things in life. You set a pace, stay in your own head, and keep moving when your body is telling you to stop. Those races were not just physical. They were a choice made over and over again. I carried that into everything else.

What drives me

I build for people, not products.

I want to build things that help people form better habits, not feed worse ones. That starts with getting close to the problem. I put myself in the position of the person dealing with it, understand what it costs them, and work from there. The problems I care about most are the ones that affect how people move through their everyday lives, not just the ones that look good on a slide deck.

The work

I turn that into things that ship.

I graduated cum laude from the University of Washington with a degree in Information Technology. My work spans product management, design, and development. I led a nonprofit website rebuild that gave staff full control of their own site. I shipped a Chrome extension from concept to working product in eight weeks. I designed an education platform from initial specs to working portals. Every project started with a real problem and a real person on the other end of it.
